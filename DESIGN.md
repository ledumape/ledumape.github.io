# Portfolio design record

## Direction

Experience mode. The portfolio is an engineering trajectory: warm drafting-paper ground, graphite navigation, solar orange for momentum, and lime for active signals. The orbit in the first viewport is the single ambient motion and projects are treated as evidence, not as a gallery of identical cards.

## Type

Unbounded carries identity and headings; Archivo carries reading; DM Mono is used only for location, labels, and concise technical metadata.

## Interaction

Project panels lift on hover, links retain standard text affordance, and all nonessential animation stops for reduced motion.
